Classes

50 classes across five categories. Every hero recruit and every Champion pull draws from this same pool — category decides which skill tree (below) a recruited hero levels into; rank sets its stat multiplier and how strong its innate signature ability rolls. Every class also carries one of five elemental types (Ember/Frost/Verdant/Umbral/Arcane), tying it to the Relic system below. The ability values shown below are Champion strength — a recruited hero's copy of the same innate rolls at 60% of it, since a hero also earns a full skill tree on top and a Champion doesn't.

Evolution — a Level 10 roster hero can spend Crystals to reforge into the next-rank class within its own role (e.g. a Rank F Squire → Rank E Duelist), rescaling its stats and swapping its innate, flavor and elemental type while keeping all accumulated level/skill progress. Cards marked "Max evolution" above sit at the top of their role's ladder.

Skill Trees

A recruited hero's category decides its tree. Every tree shares the same shape — two generic Tier-1 nodes, two category-flavored Tier-2 nodes, one capstone requiring both — so only the flavor changes per category.

Party Abilities

One free tactical choice before every Engage — unlocked the moment any hero of that role in the party reaches Level 3. Reusable every fight (not consumed permanently), but only one per fight.

Rally and Ambush are deliberately asymmetric: Rally wins on long fights (flat bonus every round), Ambush wins on short ones (a much bigger one-round spike). Neither strictly dominates the other.

Relics & Items

Two distinct loot systems, both found in Rift shops and combat rewards (a 50/50 roll between the two on most drops), independent of hero/Champion rank — both keep their own three-tier rarity. Relics are party-wide: flat DMG/Shield plus an occasional special effect, drawn from a shared pool with a limited number of equip slots. Items are hero-bound gear: two slots per roster hero (Weapon, Trinket), each a focused percentage buff assigned to one hero at a time from the Roster tab.

Relic upgrades

Any owned relic can be spent Crystals on to level up (max level 5) at the Rift Auction — each level adds +15% to its DMG, Shield, and special-effect value. Cost is 15 × rarity multiplier × current level Crystals.

Items (hero-bound gear)

Items roll into one of 3 category umbrellas, each drawing from its own stat pool, and are set by dragging a tile from the Item Bag onto a slot — either on the Roster tab or mid-rift via the "Inventory & Loadout" panel between encounters. Each item is a single percentage buff to that one hero, at meaningfully higher values than a relic special since it only affects one character. Base values below scale by the same rarity multiplier as relics (×1.0 / ×1.4 / ×1.9).

Equip slots are per-hero, not fixed: every hero has 1 Weapon slot (2 for a hand-picked list of agile "dual-wield" classes — all 10 Rogues plus Duelist, Blade Dancer, and Zealot), and a number of Gear slots — shared by Armor and Focus items interchangeably — that grows with the hero's Rank: F/E → 1, D/C → 2, B/A → 3, S → 4. Evolving a hero into a higher rank unlocks more Gear slots automatically.

Wipe-guard and Boss-alpha-strike stay Relic/Skill-only effects — kept off Items by design, since those are binary once-per-fight effects rather than a scaling stat.

Rift Detectors

A rare drop from sealing a Rift (chance scales with Detector Tuning). Sold at the Rift Auction for Coins, or consumed for a Shop Boost — guarantees an Epic Relic on the next Rift's shop node instead. Base sale price: 80c (Lesser tier) / 200c (Greater) / 450c (Ascendant), plus a 30% bonus with Black Market Clearance.

Guild Upgrades

Four branches, four nodes each, funded with Crystals from the Guild Terminal's Management tab. Most nodes cap with a one-time capstone skill. Every node now shows its current effect alongside the next level's, so a purchase visibly moves a number instead of just ticking an abstract "+X%".

Guild Tier — a free, purely-derived milestone label (Founding → Established → Renowned → Legendary Guild) based on the total levels purchased across all four branches, shown at the top of the Guild Management tab as a visible marker of overall investment.

Medical Bay (a new Guild Terminal tab, separate from the Medical Bay branch node) — a limited number of beds (1 + ⌈Medical Bay level / 2⌉) heal a downed hero in 40% of the normal time. Any downed hero without a free bed still recovers at the normal passive rate — beds are a faster lane, not a requirement. Field Triage now lives here instead of the Roster tab.

Hardcore Mode — an opt-in toggle at party assembly, available on any Rift tier. Coins, Crystals and Seal Tokens earned are ×1.5, but a wipe permanently removes every roster hero in that run's party (the Champion, being ephemeral, is unaffected either way).

Endless Rift

Unlocked from the start, no need to clear the three fixed tiers first. Starts at Ascendant's exact stats (Cycle 1) and multiplies everything — monster HP/DMG, coin/crystal ranges, base Seal Tokens, recommended power — by 1 + cycle × 0.35 every time you seal its boss. Ends only when you retreat or wipe; your best cycle reached is tracked permanently on the Rift Hall card.

The scaling is linear and unbounded by design — hero/guild power has a ceiling (level 10 cap, maxed skill trees, maxed Guild Management), so Endless is meant to eventually out-pace any roster. It's a depth-record chase, not a mode you're meant to "solve."
